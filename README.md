# Electronic Devices Sales Analysis - 755 Records

### Overview
Analysis and debugging of 755 sales records of electronic devices across multiple companies to support data-driven commercial decision-making.

### Business Problem
Inconsistent sales data and lack of clear segmentation to define effective marketing policies.

### What I Did
- **Data Cleaning:** Removed duplicates, handled nulls, standardized company & product names (755 rows)
- **EDA:** Sales trends by device type, company performance, revenue distribution
- **KPIs:** Total Sales, Average Ticket, Top 5 Best-Selling Devices, Sales by Company

### Key Insight & Solution
The segment of **young entrepreneurs** shows the highest purchase frequency and growth potential.

**Recommendation:** Increase marketing measures and tailored policies for young entrepreneurs (discounts on starter kits, social media campaigns, financing).

### Tech Stack
`Excel | Python (Pandas, NumPy) | Power BI | Data Cleaning | EDA`

### Results
Clean dataset ready for decision-making + actionable marketing strategy to boost sales in key segment.
